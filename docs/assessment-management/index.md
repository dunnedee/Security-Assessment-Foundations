# Assessment management

Before any tool is run, a professional assessment is defined, authorised,
scoped, and bounded by law and by agreement. These "soft skills" are what turn
technical ability into a trustworthy professional service — and they're exactly
where a lot of newcomers are weakest.

This section covers:

- **[The engagement lifecycle](engagement-lifecycle.md)** — the phases a test
  moves through, from first conversation to remediation support.
- **[Legal & compliance (UK)](legal-compliance.md)** — the laws and standards
  that govern what you may and may not do.
- **[Scoping & rules of engagement](scoping.md)** — defining what's tested, how,
  and within what limits.
- **[Risk management](risk-management.md)** — anticipating and reducing the risk
  that testing itself introduces.
- **[Reporting & documentation](reporting.md)** — turning findings into
  something a client can act on.

!!! quote "Why this comes first"
    A tester who finds a critical flaw but can't communicate it, or who runs a
    scan a day before it was authorised, is a liability. The business around
    the test matters as much as the test.
