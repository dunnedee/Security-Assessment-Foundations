# Information gathering

Before anything else, an assessor builds a picture of the target: what systems
exist, what the organisation exposes to the world, and where the soft edges
might be. This is reconnaissance, and it's as valuable to defenders as to
testers — because you can't reduce exposure you don't know you have.

- **[OSINT & reconnaissance](osint.md)** — the difference between passive and
  active recon, and what open sources reveal.
- **[DNS enumeration](dns-enumeration.md)** — what the naming system exposes about
  an organisation's infrastructure.
- **[Web reconnaissance](web-recon.md)** — what web servers reveal about how
  they're configured.
- **[Metadata & exposed data](metadata.md)** — the information organisations leak
  without realising.

!!! info "The defender's angle"
    Read this whole section twice: once as "what can be discovered," and once as
    "what should my organisation stop exposing." The two are the same list.
